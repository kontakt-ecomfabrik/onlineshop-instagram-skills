# Visual direction

- Use vertical 9:16 framing unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Make the first frame visually legible without audio.
- Use real product scale, color, material, packaging, and environment.
- Prefer close-ups, hands, use, movement, texture, packing, workplace, and founder footage over generic stock scenes.
- Keep each shot purposeful; avoid decorative scene changes.
- Use one readable text zone and maintain safe margins.
- Keep captions high-contrast and concise.
- Design for phone production when no production resources are specified.
- When people appear in concepts, avoid showing only one gender, age, body, or background by default.

For a faceless variant, use hands, over-the-shoulder shots, product POV, screen recording, packing, process, close-ups, or voiceover. Do not label faceless content as automatically easier or better.
