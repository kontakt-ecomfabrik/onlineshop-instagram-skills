# Quality check

Before delivery, confirm:

- The idea is recognizably about a product, shop, customer, or buying situation.
- The content has one clear job and one main message.
- The hook is specific without clickbait.
- The content answers a real question, reduces an objection, demonstrates value, or advances the buying journey.
- Features are translated into use or customer benefit without exaggeration.
- The format fits the message.
- The CTA follows naturally and is not always “buy now.”
- The caption does not repeat the Reel or Carousel.
- No facts, proof, reviews, results, or claims were invented.
- Any assumptions are visible.
- The wording sounds natural when read aloud.
- A similar recent topic, hook, example, or CTA has not been reused when a content history is available.
