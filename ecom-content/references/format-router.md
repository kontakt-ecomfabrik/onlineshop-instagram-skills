# Format router

Choose the format from the communication job:

| Job | Default format | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demonstrate use, movement, scale, texture, process, or transformation | Reel | Showing is stronger than explaining |
| Explain steps, distinctions, myths, FAQs, or a compact framework | Carousel | Readers can pause and save |
| Start a conversation, test interest, show daily proof, or handle one objection quickly | Story | Immediate and sequential |
| Add context, nuance, founder observation, evidence, or discussion to a post | Caption | Complements the asset |

Defaults:

- Reel: one idea, three hook options, spoken hook separated from on-screen hook, product or problem visible in the first two to three seconds, realistic scenes, CTA matched to the goal, and a faceless version when useful.
- Carousel: four to five slides, one job per slide, no overloaded cover, roughly three short lines or fewer per slide, final slide with a meaningful action or question.
- Story: three to five frames, one message per frame, short mobile copy, interaction only when the answer will be used.
- Caption: add information instead of repeating the asset; lead with context or tension, develop one useful point, close with one appropriate CTA.
