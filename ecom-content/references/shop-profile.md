# Shop profile

Collect only the fields needed for the current task:

- Shop or brand name
- Sales model: own shop, Etsy, Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, marketplace, B2B, or mixed
- Niche and priority product
- Verified product features, materials, use, limits, price range, and differentiators
- Target customer and buying situation
- Main problem, wish, buying motive, question, or objection
- Primary Instagram goal: reach, trust, consideration, purchase, use, retention, or learning
- Brand voice and words to avoid
- Current offer, campaign, season, or launch
- Available proof: reviews, demonstrations, tests, founder experience, production footage, FAQs
- Production constraints: face-to-camera, faceless, hands-only, product footage, photos, time, frequency

When multiple fields are missing, ask at most three grouped questions. Do not block a useful draft for nonessential gaps. Mark assumptions as assumptions and never turn them into claims.
