# Idea sources

Use the strongest available sources in this order:

1. Product facts: material, function, fit, dimensions, variants, limits, care, origin, packaging
2. Product use: setup, routine, styling, combination, storage, maintenance, troubleshooting
3. Customer language: questions, chats, emails, returns, objections, purchase reasons
4. Reviews: low ratings for friction; high ratings for valued outcomes; never fabricate or overgeneralize
5. Search behavior: queries, autocomplete, marketplace questions, social search language
6. Choice moments: comparisons, alternatives, price, suitability, size, quality, delivery, trust
7. Shop proof: process, testing, sourcing, packing, service, founder decisions
8. Buying journey: discovery, trust, consideration, purchase, use, retention
9. Commercial moments: launch, restock, bundle, season, gift occasion, limited capacity

For each idea, identify which source supports it. If no source supports a factual angle, convert it into a question or mark it as a validation need.
