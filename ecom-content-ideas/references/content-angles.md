# Content angles

Vary the reasoning, not just the wording:

- Customer question
- Problem recognition
- Desired situation
- Product demonstration
- Use case
- Feature translated into benefit
- Material or construction explanation
- Comparison criteria
- Myth or misconception, only when real
- Objection handling
- Price or value logic
- Fit, size, choice, or compatibility help
- Care and durability
- Packaging, delivery, or service
- Behind the product or process
- Founder decision with customer relevance
- Review pattern or support pattern
- Before and after, only with valid evidence and appropriate claims
- Bundle, accessory, replenishment, or cross-sell
- Post-purchase success

Do not generate several ideas that use the same customer question, claim, example, and CTA with only a different opening sentence.
