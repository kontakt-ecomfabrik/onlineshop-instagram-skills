# E-Commerce Content Ideas

Generate ideas from commerce evidence, not random trends. Make each idea specific enough that another agent can create the post without guessing its purpose.

## Build the idea base

Establish the priority product, target customer, verified features, main customer question or barrier, current content goal, and available proof. Ask at most three grouped questions when needed.

Read [references/idea-sources.md](references/idea-sources.md) to select relevant sources. Read [references/content-angles.md](references/content-angles.md) to diversify angles without drifting away from the product.

## Generate and filter

1. Summarize the working brief in no more than five lines.
2. Generate a private longlist broader than the requested quantity.
3. Remove ideas that are generic, duplicative, unsupported, or interchangeable with coach content.
4. Balance the remaining ideas across customer questions, buying phases, and formats.
5. Run the novelty check in [references/duplication-check.md](references/duplication-check.md) when prior content is available.
6. Rank the strongest ideas by strategic relevance, specificity, feasibility, and proximity to the current goal.

## Default output

When the user does not specify a schema, return ten ideas in a table:

| # | Pillar | Buying phase | Customer question | Specific angle | Hook | Best format | Proof or visual | CTA | Why it is distinct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then provide:

- **Top 3 to create first** with one-sentence reasons
- **Missing evidence** that would make any idea stronger
- **Do not repeat** list when overlap was found

## Idea rules

- Use natural customer questions before artificial “viral hooks.”
- Translate features into use, decision, or outcome; do not list specifications without meaning.
- Include sales ideas, but do not make every idea an offer.
- Do not invent reviews, demand, pain points, tests, or product benefits.
- Do not assume a trend is current; verify it when trend freshness matters and research is authorized.
- Avoid short-lived trends unless they fit the brand and product naturally.
- Keep Instagram first unless another platform is explicitly requested.
- Prefer ideas feasible with a phone, real product, packaging, hands, founder, workplace, or customer-provided material.
