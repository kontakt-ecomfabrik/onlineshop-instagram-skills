# Caption patterns

Choose one pattern based on what the asset lacks:

## Observation → meaning → question

Use for founder or category insight. Ground the observation in real shop experience.

## Customer question → nuanced answer → next step

Use for FAQ content. Add a distinction or limit not visible in the asset.

## Product detail → use → choice help

Explain why a verified material, feature, size, or construction detail matters in a real situation.

## Process → reason → trust

Explain one real sourcing, making, testing, packing, or service decision and its customer relevance.

## Objection → honest response → fit

Acknowledge a reasonable concern, give evidence, and state who the product fits or may not fit.

## Offer → relevance → factual action

State the offer, why it matters to a defined customer, and the real next step. Avoid artificial urgency.

## Post-purchase tip → mistake prevention → save

Add care, use, or maintenance guidance that protects the customer's result.
