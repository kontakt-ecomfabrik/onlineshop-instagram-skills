# Forbidden patterns

Remove or rewrite:

- Empty grand claims: “This changes everything.”
- Fake consensus: “Everyone is doing this.”
- Unsupported certainty: “This guarantees more sales.”
- Manufactured secrecy: “Nobody tells you this.”
- Generic creator bait: “Stop scrolling.”
- Shame or blame: “If your shop fails, it is because you…”
- Artificial binary choices: “Either you do this or you will never grow.”
- Repetitive “not X, but Y” constructions throughout the caption
- Excessive em dashes, fragments, emojis, bolding, or one-line paragraphs used only to imitate social-media style
- Generic AI phrases: “unlock,” “elevate,” “delve,” “in today's digital landscape,” “game changer,” “revolutionary”
- Invented personal claims: “I tested this,” “my customers always say,” or “we sold out” without evidence
- Repetition of the post's visible copy

Prefer precise nouns and verbs, real examples, and one calm conclusion.
