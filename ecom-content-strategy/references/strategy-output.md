# Strategy output

Use this structure:

## 1. Diagnosis

- Shop situation
- Main content bottleneck
- Primary audience decision
- Assumptions or evidence gaps

## 2. Priorities

- Primary goal
- Up to two supporting goals
- Priority customer-journey phases
- What not to prioritize now

## 3. Pillars

Present a table with: pillar, customer question, buying phase, job, proof, series, format, metric.

## 4. Content mix

Recommend a realistic weekly or monthly mix. Explain the logic; do not impose one universal frequency.

## 5. First actions

Give exactly three prioritized actions and a compact first-week example.

## 6. Measurement

Match metrics to jobs. For example, assess discovery through relevant reach and profile behavior, consideration through saves/questions/product visits, and purchase through attributable shop actions. Do not use likes as the default success metric.
