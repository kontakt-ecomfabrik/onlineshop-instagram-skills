# Content pillars

Create three to five pillars from the shop's evidence and buying journey. Useful families include:

- **Product understanding:** material, function, design, variations, limits, comparison
- **Problem and use:** customer situation, use case, demonstration, care, outcome
- **Trust and proof:** process, testing, founder decision, customer evidence, service
- **Buying clarity:** FAQ, fit, size, delivery, price logic, objections, choice help
- **Retention:** use after purchase, maintenance, accessories, combinations, repeat need
- **Shop perspective:** a relevant founder observation or category opinion grounded in experience

For each pillar specify:

1. Customer question
2. Buying phase
3. Business purpose
4. Evidence source
5. Two repeatable series
6. Suitable formats
7. Success signal

Avoid pillars that are only broad themes such as “motivation,” “lifestyle,” or “tips.” Make every pillar useful for generating specific shop content.
