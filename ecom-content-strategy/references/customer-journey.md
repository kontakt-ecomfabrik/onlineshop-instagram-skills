# Customer journey for shop content

| Phase | Customer state | Useful content jobs | Example signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Does not yet know the shop or fully name the need | Make a problem, wish, use case, or product category recognizable | Relevant reach, profile visits, qualified follows |
| Trust | Wonders whether the shop and product are credible | Show material, process, people, proof, limitations, service, and consistency | Saves, profile depth, replies, repeat viewers |
| Consideration | Compares options and evaluates fit | Answer FAQs, objections, comparison criteria, sizing, care, price logic, and use | Saves, shares, product-page visits, questions |
| Purchase | Needs clarity and a reason to act | Present the offer, bundle, availability, delivery, guarantee terms, or launch with factual urgency | Product clicks, carts, attributed orders |
| Use | Wants a good result after purchase | Explain setup, care, styling, troubleshooting, and best use | Fewer support questions, saves, positive replies |
| Retention | May buy again, recommend, or deepen use | Show refills, accessories, combinations, replenishment, community, and customer care | Repeat engagement, repeat orders, referrals |

Choose phases from the shop's actual bottleneck. Do not treat the journey as a rigid funnel; customers may enter at different points.
