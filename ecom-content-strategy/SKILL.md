# E-Commerce Content Strategy

Create a usable Instagram strategy for a shop. Base it on products, customer decisions, available proof, business priorities, and realistic production capacity.

## Gather the minimum context

Establish:

- Priority product or category
- Target customer and buying situation
- Main buying motive and main barrier
- Shop differentiation and evidence
- Commercial or communication priority for the planning period
- Current Instagram capacity and usable assets
- Existing content that performed well or should not be repeated

Ask at most three grouped questions when essential facts are missing. Label assumptions.

## Map the buying journey

Read [references/customer-journey.md](references/customer-journey.md). Select the two or three phases most important now; do not distribute posts evenly by habit.

## Build the strategy

1. Diagnose the main content problem in one sentence.
2. Define one primary goal and up to two supporting goals.
3. Define three to five content pillars using [references/content-pillars.md](references/content-pillars.md).
4. Assign each pillar a customer question, buying phase, content job, proof source, and suitable formats.
5. Set a realistic cadence from the user's capacity. Do not impose daily posting or a fixed Reel quota.
6. Balance discovery, trust, consideration, purchase, and retention according to the diagnosis.
7. Define what to measure for each goal. Distinguish leading indicators from sales outcomes.
8. Produce the output using [references/strategy-output.md](references/strategy-output.md).

## Strategic rules

- Strategy precedes the calendar. Do not fill dates before priorities and pillars are clear.
- Treat reach as useful only when it attracts relevant potential customers.
- Connect educational content to a product, buying decision, use case, or shop advantage.
- Include sales content without turning every post into a promotion.
- Use founder content only when it supports trust, differentiation, or customer understanding.
- Use AI as a production aid, never as the content angle by default.
- Do not promise sales or growth from posting frequency alone.
- Keep Instagram as the scope unless the user explicitly requests other channels.

## Quality gate

Before delivery, verify that the strategy:

- could not be copied unchanged to an unrelated shop;
- covers the most relevant customer-journey gaps;
- includes evidence and product reality;
- is feasible with the user's stated time and assets;
- gives clear priorities rather than a long unranked list;
- states assumptions and data gaps transparently.
