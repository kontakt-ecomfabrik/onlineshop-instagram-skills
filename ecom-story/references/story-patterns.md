# Story patterns

## Question → answer → next step

Use for FAQs and objections. Start with the customer's exact question, answer clearly, then link or invite a relevant reply.

## Problem → demonstration → result

Use when the problem and product use can be shown honestly. Avoid exaggerated before-and-after framing.

## Behind the scenes → decision → customer meaning

Show a real process or founder choice, then explain why it matters to the customer.

## Choice help → poll → recommendation

Ask a meaningful preference question, then explain what the answer changes. Plan a follow-up with the result.

## Launch or restock

State what is available, who it fits, the key verified details, and the factual action. Use genuine date or stock information only.

## Proof sequence

Show the claim, the evidence, its limits, and the customer relevance. Do not use anonymous invented quotes.

## Post-purchase help

Teach setup, care, combination, maintenance, or troubleshooting. End with a save, reply, or support step.
