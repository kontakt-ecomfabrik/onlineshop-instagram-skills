# Carousel patterns

## Four-slide explanation

1. Specific question or tension
2. What is commonly misunderstood
3. Clear explanation or method
4. Action or customer question

## Five-slide buying guide

1. Buying question
2. Criterion one
3. Criterion two
4. How to choose based on situation
5. Next step

## Five-slide objection handler

1. Honest objection
2. Why the concern is reasonable
3. Relevant evidence or distinction
4. Who the product fits and does not fit
5. Decision prompt

## Five-slide product-development insight

1. Product challenge
2. Customer signal or review pattern
3. What to examine
4. Concrete improvement categories
5. Validation action

## Five-slide product-use sequence

1. Desired use or result
2. Preparation
3. Correct use
4. Common mistake or care note
5. Save or try action

The cover must state a useful promise, question, or tension without hiding the topic. Each following slide should answer the previous slide's implied question.
